Product Demand & Inventory Analysis

📌 **Project Overview**

Nova Retail Solutions wanted to better understand its product demand, sales value and warehouse performance. The goal of this analysis was to identify demand patterns, evaluate product and category performance, compare warehouse performance and uncover trends that could support better inventory and business decisions.

## Data preparation

The raw dataset was prepared in Power Query before building the Power BI data model.

- Reviewed the dataset structure and column types
- Converted the Date field into the appropriate date format and validated the Time field
- Checked for errors and empty values after data type transformations
- Reviewed key fields: Order Demand, Order Value, Product, Category and Warehouse
- Loaded the cleaned dataset into Power BI for modelling and analysis

### Dataset

Approximately 46,000 orders covering 2018–2019. Key fields:

- Category, Product, Warehouse
- Zip Code
- Date and Time
- Order Demand
- Order Value

🔍 Key Insights

- Total order demand was approximately 1.68 billion, indicating a high volume of product demand across the analyzed data.
- Total order value was approximately 14.93 million.
- The dataset contained approximately 46,000 orders.
- Average order demand was approximately 36.66K.
- Average order value was approximately 326.15.
- Overall demand declined by approximately 37.67%, indicating a significant reduction in demand over the analyzed period.
- Warehouse performance varied across locations, with some warehouses generating higher revenue despite not having the highest order demand.
- The analysis also identified products with relatively high demand but lower revenue contribution, highlighting areas that may require further investigation.

💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Investigate the decline in demand.** The 37.67% decline from 2018 to 2019 should be investigated to identify underlying causes such as changes in customer behavior, product performance, pricing or market conditions.
2. **Prioritize high-demand products.** Maintain adequate inventory levels for TBOX to reduce the risk of stockouts and ensure continued availability.
3. **Analyze high-revenue products.** Products such as VideoSlight should be analyzed further to understand what drives their strong revenue contribution and where growth opportunities lie.
4. **Optimize warehouse performance.** Evaluate warehouses on both demand and revenue, not demand volume alone, and use those metrics to improve inventory allocation and operational efficiency.
5. **Monitor demand trends regularly.** Use the Power BI dashboard to continuously monitor demand, revenue, orders, products and warehouses so that changes are identified and addressed quickly.
